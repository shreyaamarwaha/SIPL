About SIPL

Deep research, translated into clinical-grade AI.

Sequoia Insilico is a New Delhi-based BioAI company advancing mental and brain health. LifeBack®, our flagship, brings population-scale insight with individual precision at every stage of the treatment cycle—triage, point of care, prognosis, and risk assessment.

Trust

Responsible by design.

AI supports the clinician. The clinician leads the care. Safeguards are built in from day one — not added later.

01

Honest science

We start with a question, not a dataset.

  • Question firstEvery model begins with a clear, testable hypothesis.
  • Ethically approvedStudies follow ICMR guidelines and ethics review.
  • Open about limitsWe say where a model works — and where it doesn’t.

02

Protected data

Patient information is used only as agreed.

  • Consent, alwaysData is used only for the purpose a patient agreed to.
  • Right people onlyAccess is limited by role and every action is logged.
  • DPDP Act, 2023Built to meet India’s data protection law.

03

Safe to use

Proven before it reaches a patient.

  • Clinician decidesAI suggests; the clinician holds the final call.
  • Medical-device readyFollowing CDSCO’s Software as a Medical Device path.
  • Checked for lifeValidated before launch and monitored after.
