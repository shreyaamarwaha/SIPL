About SIPL

Deep research, translated into clinical-grade AI.

Sequoia Insilico Pvt. Ltd. is a deep-science BioAI company in New Delhi. Our work is grounded in research across bioinformatics, systems biology, and computational psychiatry.

Each finding follows one path: researched, validated in hospital cohorts, and deployed as a clinical-grade model. LifeBack™ is the first platform built this way. Our long-term aim is to redefine psychiatric diagnosis through a population-scale atlas of phenotypes, biotypes, and treatment outcomes.

The SIPL architecture

One loop, not three tools.

Care is delivered, integrated into hospital workflow, and continuously improved by discovery — one connected system.

Care

The clinical engine — assessment, scoring, and monitoring that clinicians use directly.

Integration

Care built into hospital workflows and records, so it runs inside existing practice, not alongside it.

Discovery

New findings — from our R&D, patient-reported outcomes, and outside research — validated and folded back into care.

Our precision framework

Precision care must be multidimensional.

At the point of care, precision is ultimately delivered to the individual patient. But true individual precision depends on two equally important foundations.

Diagnostic precision

Understanding overlapping disorders, comorbidities, differential diagnoses, and disease trajectories accurately — rather than treating conditions as isolated labels.

Population precision

Ensuring models are relevant to the demographic, genetic, linguistic, cultural, and socioeconomic diversity of the population in which they are used.

LifeBack™ brings these dimensions together — so population-relevant, diagnostically precise intelligence can be translated into truly individualised care.

LifeBack™ platform

Built to deploy anywhere, engineered for India first.

LifeBack™ is SIPL’s flagship clinical-grade platform for brain and mental health — requiring clinical intervention, not a self-serve consumer app — designed for B2B and B2B2C deployment with white-label integration for hospitals, healthcare providers, corporates, and institutions. Built with leading hospitals and clinical KOLs (Key Opinion Leaders), it combines demographically validated deep learning models with rigorous patient-centred clinical research and a strong translational science foundation.

Built for diversity, adaptable globally

A use-case–specific small language model for India's diverse, heterogeneous population — adaptable globally. SaaS, API-first: pilot in weeks with no IT change; production-ready, integrating into existing HIS/EMR.

Across the patient journey

Clinical workflows, multimodal data, and biological insights follow the patient across risk, diagnosis, response, prognosis, remission, and relapse — with adaptive evaluation, clinician-led planning, and long-term surveillance. A patient app links PROs to clinician dashboards between visits, extending post-remission and relapse analytics to patients, not just clinicians.

A collaboration platform

Backed by strong R&D and translational research, SIPL partners with hospitals, research institutions, and biopharma across brain health, mental health, and precision medicine — through co-creation, joint publications, co-hosted conferences, and patent partnerships, licensing complementary IP to strengthen services like Data-as-a-Service (DaaS).
